How it works

From raw clips to publish-ready in three steps.

Step 1

Send your footage

Drop your clips in the private upload link and fill a ten-minute brief: the platform, the goal, and any moments you know are gold.

Step 2

We cut and polish

Your editor trims the dead air, grades the colour, adds captions and sound design, and paces the edit for the platform it's going to.

Step 3

Review and publish

You get a review link with timestamped comments. Two revision rounds are included, then the final files land in your inbox ready to post.

FAQ

The questions everyone asks before booking.

What if I don't like the edit?

Two revision rounds are built into every plan — leave timestamped comments on the review link and we act on them within 48 hours. And your first video is covered by the watch-it-again guarantee above: re-edit free, or a full refund if it still misses the brief.

How fast will I actually get my video?

Single Cuts deliver in five business days. Creator Plan clients get 72-hour priority turnaround per video. If we ever miss a promised date, that video is half price — it's happened twice, both hurricanes.

Do I need fancy footage or gear?

No. Phone footage is most of what we cut. What matters is decent audio and enough coverage — the brief walks you through both, and we'll flag anything unusable before we start, not after.

Who owns the final videos and my raw footage?

You do — fully, forever, including the project files if you ask. We keep your raw footage on file for 90 days in case you want recuts, then delete it unless you tell us otherwise.
